My story

Three cities, three very different chapters.

Paris · 2017–2020

Performance

I started in luxury retail and client service, at Chanel and Zadig & Voltaire. I liked the challenge: targets, KPIs, and being measured on results every day. I became the store's top sales performer and brought in 16% of its revenue.

Seoul · 2021–2026

The leap

In October 2020 I found out about a five-year full scholarship to study in Seoul. I knew almost nothing about Korea, applied in less than a week, and two months later learned I was one of five people selected worldwide that year. In February 2021 I moved and started again from zero.

At Hankuk University of Foreign Studies I discovered international relations, development economics, technology policy and global health, and how much I like understanding complex systems. After graduating, I joined Thales Korea to see how a complex, regulated, government-driven market works in practice.

Gothenburg · 2026–

Going quantitative

I chose public health science in Gothenburg to learn what I'd never studied: epidemiology, biostatistics and the methods behind health decisions. A new country and a new culture again, on purpose. My goal is the health economics track and a quantitative approach to HTA and health innovation.
